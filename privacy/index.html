Who runs this site

This website (www.9a3why.space and its subdomains) is a private, non-commercial hobby site run by Amir Jasarevic, amateur radio call sign 9A3WHY, in Croatia. I am the person responsible for the data described here (the "controller" in the sense of the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).

Contact for anything about privacy: 9a3why [at] gmail [dot] com

The short version

  • There are no forms, accounts, comments or newsletters. You cannot type anything into this site, so I receive nothing you write.
  • The pages load nothing from other companies: no analytics, no advertising, no social media buttons, no external fonts or scripts.
  • The site sets no cookies of its own and does not store anything in your browser.
  • I keep no access logs of my own. The site is delivered by Cloudflare; details below.
  • Your data is not sold, rented or shared for marketing.

Logs and statistics

The site is a set of plain files that Cloudflare stores and delivers from its own network. I do not run a web server for it, and I keep no access logs of my own: I do not store visitors' IP addresses, page requests or browser details.

Through my Cloudflare account I can see aggregated traffic figures (for example the number of requests and the countries they come from) and security events such as blocked bots, which can include IP addresses. I use them only to keep the site secure, find faults and block abuse. This is my legitimate interest under GDPR Article 6(1)(f). I do not export them, link them to individuals or build profiles of visitors, and Cloudflare deletes them on its own schedule.

Who sees them: only me. I pass such data on only if it is needed to deal with an attack or if the law requires it.

Cloudflare

The site is stored on and delivered through Cloudflare (HTTPS, caching and protection against attacks). Your requests therefore pass through Cloudflare's network, which processes technical data such as your IP address and request details, and may use technical cookies for security. This happens under Cloudflare's own terms and privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. Cloudflare may process data outside the EU, using the safeguards described there.

The source files of the site are kept in a public GitHub repository (github.com/why0712/www.9a3why.space). It contains only the published pages and images and no visitor data.

Links to other sites

The pages link to other websites, such as photo services (Pixieset, Fotozine, 500px), GitHub, Meetup, CroMesh.eu and Radio Klub Karlovac. Once you click a link you are on their site and their privacy policy applies, not this one.

Private tools

Some subdomains of this site are private tools for the site owner and require a login (protected by Cloudflare Access). They are not open to visitors and are not used to collect data about visitors.

Google user data (backup tool)

I use a personal backup tool, called why-intel backup and based on the open-source program rclone, to keep encrypted backups of my own server in my own Google Drive. To do this it asks Google for permission through Google sign-in (OAuth). This tool is for my own use only: no visitor or other person ever signs in to it, and it handles no one else's Google data.

  • What it accesses: only files and folders that the tool itself creates in my Google Drive (the Google permission "drive.file"). It cannot see my other Drive files.
  • What it does: it uploads backup archives of my server settings and data. The contents and the file names are encrypted on my server before upload.
  • Where it is stored: in my Google Drive, and the sign-in token on my own server.
  • Sharing: the data is not shared, sold or transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to develop or train AI models.
  • Revoking access: I can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the backups from my Drive.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask what data I hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, and to complain to the data protection authority. In Croatia that is the Agencija za zastitu osobnih podataka (AZOP), azop.hr. Because I keep no access logs and nothing on the site is linked to an account, I usually hold no personal data about you beyond what you send me by email. For the technical data that Cloudflare processes, Cloudflare is responsible, as described above. Write to 9a3why [at] gmail [dot] com.

Children

This site is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Changes

If something changes, for example I add a service that collects data, I will update this page and its date before it takes effect.
